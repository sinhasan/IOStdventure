# Digital Colleagues

Digital Colleagues are role-based autonomous workers inside TD Venture IOS.

## Chief of Staff

Coordinates all Digital Colleagues.

## Investment Analyst

Analyzes startups, investors, sectors, and opportunities.

## Opportunity Manager

Moves opportunities through the lifecycle.

## Communications Officer

Queues, sends, and tracks communications.

## Meeting Coordinator

Schedules meetings and manages reminders.

## Portfolio Analyst

Tracks funded companies and portfolio performance.

## Knowledge Officer

Maintains institutional memory.
