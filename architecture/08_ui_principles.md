# UI Principles

1. Every screen answers one business question.
2. UI should show what happens next.
3. Users should feel they are moving opportunities, not filling forms.
4. Protected identity must be clearly explained.
5. Bloomberg-style density is acceptable only when clarity remains high.
6. Green indicates live operations.
7. Every workflow should show current stage and next action.

## Key Screens

Dashboard:
What needs attention?

AI Matches:
Who should I contact?

Opportunities:
Where is every active opportunity?

Chief of Staff:
What should I do today?
