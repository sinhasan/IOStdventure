# TD Venture IOS Constitution

## Identity

TD Venture IOS means Investment Operating System.

It is not a CRM, not a marketplace, and not a chatbot.

It is the operating system for modern investment organizations.

## Mission

Enable investment organizations to make better decisions, operate more efficiently, and increase the probability that deserving founders receive funding.

## Core Principle

Humans provide judgment. Digital Colleagues execute.

## Business Object

Opportunity is the core business object.

Every match, communication, meeting, payment, document, due diligence action, and portfolio activity exists to move an Opportunity forward.

## Engineering Principles

1. Engines before screens.
2. Business Events before CRUD.
3. Every important action leaves a timeline.
4. No hidden workflows.
5. No duplicated business logic.
6. Every AI recommendation must be explainable.
7. Every engine has one clear owner.
8. Every workflow must define human escalation.
9. Build for 2035, not only 2026.
10. No shortcuts.

## North Star

Every line of code should help investment organizations operate better and increase the probability that deserving founders get funded.
