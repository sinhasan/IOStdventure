# Business Engines

Every engine owns one business capability.

## Opportunity Engine

Mission:
Move every opportunity through a disciplined lifecycle.

Owns:
- Opportunity creation
- Duplicate prevention
- Opportunity IDs
- State transitions
- Owner assignment
- Next action

Does not own:
- Email delivery
- Payments
- Meetings
- Documents

## Communications Engine

Mission:
Ensure every participant receives timely, professional and traceable communication.

Owns:
- Email queue
- Email templates
- Delivery worker
- Retry logic

## Chief of Staff Engine

Mission:
Prioritize what matters and escalate what needs human judgment.

Owns:
- Daily briefing
- Priorities
- Escalations
- Opportunity health
